The mean from this given class-interval frequency table:

$$
Mean = \bar{X} = \frac{\sum \text{Frequency}\times \text{Mid-point}}{\text{Total Frequencies}}=\frac{137.5}{30}=4.58\bar{3}
$$


The **median group** is $3.95-4.95$.
To get the the estimated median, we have this formula:

$$
\text{Estimated Median} = L + \frac{(\frac{n}{2})-B}{f_m}\times w
$$
$L: \text{Lower class boundary of the median group}$
$n: \text{Total frequencies}$
$f_m: \text{Frequency of the median group}$
$B: \text{Total frequencies before the median group}$
$w: \text{Group width}$

$L = 3.95$
$n = 30$
$f_m = 10$
$B = 11$
$w = 1$

$$
\text{Estimated Median} = 3.95 + \frac{(\frac{30}{2})-11}{10}\times 1 = 4.35
$$

The **modal group** is $3.95-4.95$.
To get the the estimated mode, we have this formula:

$$
\text{Estimated Mode} = L+\frac{f_{m}-f_{m-1}}{(f_{m}-f_{m-1})+(f_{m}-f_{m+1})}\times w
$$

$L: \text{Lower class boundary of the modal group}$
$f_{m-1}: \text{Frequency of the group before the modal group}$
$f_{m}: \text{Frequency of the modal group}$
$f_{m+1}: \text{Frequency of the group after the modal group}$
$w: \text{Group width}$

$L = 3.95$
$f_{m-1} = 8$
$f_m = 10$
$f_{m+1} = 9$
$w = 1$

$$
\text{Estimated Mode} = 3.95+\frac{10-8}{(10-8)+(10+9)}\times 1 = 4.0452
$$
