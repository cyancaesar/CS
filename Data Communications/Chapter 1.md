What is *Data*?
- Data: raw facts, concepts and instructions that collected.
- Information: processed data that enable us to take decisions.

What is *Communication*?
- Communication: the process of exchanging information via a specific transmission medium.
- Telecommunication: implies the exchange of information over a long distance.

What is *Data Communications*?
- Data communications: the process of communicating information in binary form between two points.
- Called digital communications or computer communications.

What is *Data Communication System*?
- Electronic equipment assembled together form a data communication system.
- Hardware: sending and receiving devices.
- Software: a protocol that specifies the rules governing the communication process.

Data Types and Digitization

Types of data:
- Text
- Number
- Image
- Audio
- Video

1. Text
	1. a sequence of symbols used to represent an idea.
	2. ASCII, Extended ASCII, EBCDIC, Unicode, ISO code
2. Number
	1. Represented using the binary system
3. Image
	1. Encode description in terms of 1s and 0s
4. Audio
	1. Continuous, not discrete
	2. To get the digital format, sequence of operations:
		1. Sampling
		2. Quantization
		3. Encoding to binary patterns
5. Video
	1. Represented by images (frames) in times
	2. Combination of images represents the video
