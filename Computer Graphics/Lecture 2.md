# Display Hardware


- Referred ad Video Display Unit (VDU)
- Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) is the popular
- Others
	- CRT
	- Plasma
	- Field Emission Displays
	- Light Emitting Diodes (LED)
	- Hologram

## Raster Scan Display

- Raster: A rectangular array of points or dots
- Screen is a regular grid of samples called pixels
- Interlacing: Technique to avoid flickering effect on smaller refresh rates

## Random Scan Display

- Raster Scan represents images as a collection of pixels
- Random Scan draw a picture one line at a time
